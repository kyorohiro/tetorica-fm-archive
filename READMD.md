Tetorica FM Archive

An archive of FM synthesis research, analysis, patches, and musical experiments.

This repository collects notes and data created while studying FM sound sources, including the YM2612.

Contents

* FM sound analysis
* Instrument and patch data
* Music analysis
* Playground examples
* Research notes
